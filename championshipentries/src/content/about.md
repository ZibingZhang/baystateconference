# About

**ChampionshipEntries** is a tool for building championship swim meet entries: athlete rosters, individual entries, and relay entries, organized by meet, ready to export as a HY3 file for Hy-Tek Meet Manager.

See the **How To** page for a walkthrough of setting up a meet, importing events, and exporting entries.

## Data & privacy

All of your data — meets, athletes, and entries — is stored only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and nothing is shared between devices or browsers. Clearing your browser's site data will erase everything, so use Export CSV (or Export to HY3) to keep a backup of anything important.
