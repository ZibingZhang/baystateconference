# About

**ChampionshipEntries** is a tool for building championship swim meet entries: athlete rosters, individual entries, and relay entries, organized by meet, ready to export as a HY3 file for Hy-Tek Meet Manager.

See the **How To** page for a walkthrough of setting up a meet, importing events, and exporting entries.

## Data & privacy

All of your data — meets, athletes, and entries — is stored only in this browser's local storage.
Nothing is uploaded to a server, and nothing is shared between devices or browsers.
See **Data & privacy** on the **How To** page for how to back up and restore it.
