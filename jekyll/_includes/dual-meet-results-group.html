Shared by the current dual-meets/results/ page (scoped to the current school year) and the generated per-school-year historical pages (see _plugins/dual_meet_results_page_generator.rb), so both stay in sync. A meet's outcome comes from its Team 1/Team 2 Score when both are known, falling back to the Team 1 Result (W/L/T) column for older meets where only the outcome - not the actual score - was recorded.
