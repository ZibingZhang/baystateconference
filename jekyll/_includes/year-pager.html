{%- comment -%} Renders Previous/Next links between a generated per-school-year page's neighboring years, e.g. dual meet results, rankings, qualifying standards, and all-star pages. Expects `previous_url`/`previous_title` and `next_url`/`next_title`; either pair may be omitted at either end of the range, in which case that link is simply not rendered. Optionally also expects `oldest_url`/`oldest_title` and `latest_url`/`latest_title`, rendered as `<<`/`>>` jump links outside the Previous/Next pair, for skipping straight to the oldest/most current year instead of stepping one at a time. Callers should omit these (nil) on the page that's already that year, the same way previous/next are omitted at the ends of the range. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if include.previous_url or include.next_url -%}
