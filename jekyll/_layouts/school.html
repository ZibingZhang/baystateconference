--- layout: base --- {%- comment -%} Renders a single school's page: a card with its logo and a 2-column info table, built from its entry in _data/schools.yaml. Sport-agnostic - a page can set `sport_slug` in its front matter to also render that school's `sport.` data generically: - any ungrouped key (see sport-info-rows.html) - so a plain sport- specific fact just needs a key in schools.yaml, no partial to write - a `sections` key (see sport-info-sections.html) - named groups of facts (e.g. "Girls"/"Boys"), each rendered under its own header row A page whose section needs something schools.yaml can't express (e.g. swimming's historical win counts, which come from results data rather than a plain schools.yaml value) can additionally set `section_extra` to the path of a partial appending more rows to a given section - see swimming-diving-school-section-extra.html. `info_extra` remains as a fallback for anything ungrouped and not expressible via schools.yaml either, rendered as further extra rows after everything else. Instagram accounts are handled separately from the rest of `sport.`: they live in a flat, tagged `instagram` list at the top of the school's schools.yaml entry (see that file's comment), and get filtered into department/sport/section rows by instagram_rows_filter.rb - see its comment and instagram-rows.html for why that's Ruby rather than more nested Liquid. A sport whose only sectioned content is Instagram doesn't need its own `sport..sections` stub in schools.yaml at all - instagram_section_names derives the Girls/Boys split straight from whichever genders show up in that sport's Instagram entries. Declare `sections` explicitly (as swim does) only when a section needs something beyond Instagram - e.g. swim's win-count section_extra, which can apply to a gender with no Instagram account - since that always wins over the derived fallback. A page using this layout needs `school: "Braintree"` in its front matter to match against the `town` field in schools.yaml, and may optionally set `sport_slug: "baseball"`, `section_extra: "some-partial.html"`, and/or `info_extra: "some-partial.html"`. When `sport_slug` is set, the schedule link/notice below is scoped to that one sport rather than the school's schedule overall: a current-season schedule for it (school_schedule_page_generator.rb's schools_with_current_season_sport_schedule) links straight there, otherwise a plain notice says there's no current-season schedule for it. That's deliberately as far as this goes - there's no participation flag anywhere (schools.yaml's `sport.` map is just hand-authored extras) to say whether the school fields the sport at all, so the notice doesn't claim one way or the other, just that this season's schedule isn't here. Without `sport_slug`, it's the simpler school-wide check this always had. {%- endcomment -%}
