The Bay State Conference comprises of 10 high schools in two divisions, divided by geography: the Carey Division (Brookline, Framingham, Natick, Newton North, and Wellesley) and the Herget Division (Braintree, Milton, Needham, Walpole, and Weymouth).[^1]
