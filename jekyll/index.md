This is an unofficial hub for Bay State Conference athletics, built to make it easier to follow the ten schools that make up the league. The Bay State Conference is split into two divisions: the Carey Division (Brookline, Framingham, Natick, Newton North, and Wellesley) and the Herget Division (Braintree, Milton, Needham, Walpole, and Weymouth). Across those schools, the conference fields teams in dozens of sports, from football and basketball to track and field, swimming, and golf. Here you'll find schedules and results for every game, updated daily, along with sport-specific pages that break down standings, rosters, and season history by school.
