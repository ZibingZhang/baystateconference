As the EMISCA has grown and worked tirelessly to foster positive relations with the EMSOA, the __Priscilla Davis Award__ was established in 2014 as a special recognition of an EMSOA official who was inducted into our Hall of Fame. Priscilla continued to give countless hours of time to the sport of swimming and diving with a whistle in her hand and a smile on her face. Her willingness to mentor new officials, as well as to softly educate new coaches on the ins and outs of swimming and diving is something we all strive for. Everyone knew (from the coaches, to meet directors, to athletes, and fans) that when Priscilla was on the deck, things would run fairly and smoothly. She always had the athlete's best interest at heart. In the spirit of her name, the EMISCA honors someone from the swimming and diving community each year who has served with Priscilla's ideals in mind for at least 25 years.
