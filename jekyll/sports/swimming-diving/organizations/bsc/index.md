The __Bay State Conference__ comprises of 10 high schools in two divisions, divided by geography: the Carey Division (Brookline, Framingham, Natick, Newton North, and Wellesley) and the Herget Division (Braintree, Milton, Needham, Walpole, and Weymouth).[^1]

Girls swimming and diving is a fall sport, while boys swimming and diving is a winter sport. Four out of the ten high schools don't have a boys team in the winter: Braintree, Milton, Walpole, and Weymouth.
